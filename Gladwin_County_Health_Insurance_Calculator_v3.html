Methodology & notes
• Employee annual costs are the hard-cap annual employee contributions provided for Gladwin County. Negative amounts (green) are dollars back to the employee and reduce total cost.
• Plans: Priority Health HSA 2500 PH01 and HSA 3000 PH04 (Priority Health Schedules of Benefits, effective 1/1/2026); BCBSM Value 1500 205 and Value HSA 2500 212/213 (BCBSM Benefits-at-a-Glance, effective 1/1/2026).
• Medical costs assume one $150 office visit per $500 of medical cost (up to 12 visits). On Value 1500 205, office visits are a $30 copay not subject to the deductible; all other medical costs go to the deductible, then coinsurance up to the coinsurance maximum. On the HSA plans, all medical costs apply to the deductible first.
• Rx is modeled at the per-fill maximum: generic $10; preferred brand/specialty $80; non-preferred brand/specialty $160 on Priority Health plans and $100 on BCBSM plans. On HSA plans, before the deductible is met each fill costs 3x that amount (applied to the deductible); after the deductible, the regular amount applies. Value 1500 205 Rx is not subject to the deductible.
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage. Rx counts toward the out-of-pocket maximum.
• Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included. Estimates are for illustration only; actual costs depend on claims and the plan documents.
